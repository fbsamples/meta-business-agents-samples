Tell the customer where their refund is.

1. Identify the order (ask for the number or call `list_orders`), then call `get_refund_status`.
2. Report the status, amount and date from the result. If the refund was issued, say it can take the time stated in the return policy to reach their account.
3. If there is no refund on the order, say so and ask whether they have started a return. If not, offer to help with that.
4. If a refund is overdue by the policy's timeline, apologize and hand off to the team.
